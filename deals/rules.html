Rules

outdeals is one public leaderboard for retail discounts. There are no ads, no affiliate cuts, and no ranking algorithm. You pay to stand above every other deal on the board. Rank is the bid — nothing else.

How ranking works

  • One board. Every deal — Amazon, Target, Walmart, Altamuta — files into the same ranking and competes with all the others. Each listing is tagged with the retailer board it came from and its rank within that board, so Amazon #2 tells you where a deal stands among Amazon deals while its big number tells you where it stands overall. The retailer tabs filter the board; they do not create separate contests.
  • New listings are whole US dollars, $5 minimum, $999,999 maximum, $1 at a time. Bids already on the board keep their amount until they raise or get outranked.
  • Taking #1 costs at least $5 more than the current top bid. Paying less still puts you on the board at whatever rank that bid can take. Equal bids stay in the order they were placed — the older bid keeps the higher rank.
  • Enter the same deal link again to raise that listing to any rank. The new bid must be at least $1 above your current bid; you only pay the difference. Someone else cannot take your rank by paying that difference — they pay their own bid in full.
  • Product links are keyed by their path, so two different deals from the same retailer never share a bid. Tracking query strings (utm_*, tag, ref, gclid and similar) are stripped before the link is matched, and the rules are enforced on the server — the page cannot talk it into a cheaper spot.

What you can list

  • A product or deal page on Amazon, Target, Walmart, or Altamuta.
  • One listing per product page. Bundles, storefronts, and category pages are fine as long as the link points at a real, live offer.
  • Search result pages, cart links, coupon-code walls, and chat or invite links are not allowed. The board is for deals, not funnels.

What gets removed

  • Dead links, expired promotions, and prices that no longer match what the listing claims.
  • Fake discounts — an inflated "list price" used to manufacture a percentage off.
  • Malware, scams, adult content, and anything illegal to sell.
  • Removals are not refunded. Check your link before you bid.

Money

  • Bids are one-time payments, not subscriptions. Nothing renews and nothing is charged again unless you raise your own bid.
  • Nothing reaches the board until the payment clears. Your bid is held as pending while you are at the payment page and is applied only when our payment provider confirms the charge.
  • If the board moves while you are paying, you are refunded in full. A payment buys exactly the position it was quoted for. If someone else lists or raises that same deal between your quote and your payment, the bid is refunded automatically rather than applied at a price you did not agree to.
  • Bids are otherwise non-refundable — being outranked is the product working as designed, not a fault.
  • Prices shown on a listing are supplied by whoever placed the bid. Always confirm the real price at the retailer before you buy.
