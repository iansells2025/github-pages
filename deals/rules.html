Rules

outdeals is a public leaderboard for retail discounts. There are no ads, no affiliate cuts, and no ranking algorithm. You pay to stand above every other deal on the board. Rank is the bid — nothing else.

How ranking works

  • Each retailer has its own board: Amazon, Target, Walmart and Altamuta. A deal only competes against other deals from the same retailer.
  • New listings are whole US dollars, $5 minimum, $999,999 maximum, $1 at a time. Bids already on a board keep their amount until they raise or get outranked.
  • Taking #1 costs at least $5 more than the current top bid on that board. Paying less still puts you on the board at whatever rank that bid can take. Equal bids stay in the order they were placed — the older bid keeps the higher rank.
  • Enter the same deal link again to raise that listing to any rank. The new bid must be at least $1 above your current bid; you only pay the difference. Someone else cannot take your rank by paying that difference — they pay their own bid in full.
  • Product links are keyed by their path, so two different deals from the same retailer never share a bid. Tracking query strings (utm_*, tag, ref, and similar) are stripped before the link is matched.

What you can list

  • A product or deal page on Amazon, Target, Walmart, or Altamuta.
  • One listing per product page. Bundles, storefronts, and category pages are fine as long as the link points at a real, live offer.
  • Search result pages, cart links, coupon-code walls, and chat or invite links are not allowed. The board is for deals, not funnels.

What gets removed

  • Dead links, expired promotions, and prices that no longer match what the listing claims.
  • Fake discounts — an inflated "list price" used to manufacture a percentage off.
  • Malware, scams, adult content, and anything illegal to sell.
  • Removals are not refunded. Check your link before you bid.

Money

  • Bids are one-time payments, not subscriptions. Nothing renews and nothing is charged again unless you raise your own bid.
  • Bids are non-refundable — being outranked is the product working as designed, not a fault.
  • Prices shown on a listing are supplied by whoever placed the bid. Always confirm the real price at the retailer before you buy.

This build

This is a demonstration clone. The board runs entirely in your browser: listings and bids are saved to localStorage, so what you place is visible only to you and disappears when you clear site data.
